- **Bosses turn on every V1.** A boss goes after whoever hurts it most and is close, switches targets now and then, and leaves a downed player alone. Players who aren't the target still see its health bar.
- **See your teammates.** Every other player's name, health and distance show over ULTRAKILL's view, and at the edge of the screen when they're out of sight. You can turn this off in the settings.
- **Revive by standing near.** A downed teammate's spot is marked with green light. Stand there for 4 seconds (2 with two people) to bring them back. While you're down, the camera orbits a teammate; click to switch between them.
- **Co-op Cyber Grind.** Press Join on any shop's Cyber Grind page, or use `/uc grind join`. Each extra V1 makes the waves bigger. If you go down, a teammate can revive you, or you get back up when the wave is cleared. If everyone goes down, the run ends and nobody dies. `/uc grind leave` takes you out, and if the player who started the run leaves, the next player takes it over.
- **Duels.** `/uc duel <player>` challenges another V1 to a fight.
- **Giving P.** `/uc p give <player> <amount>` sends some of your P to another player.
- **A weaker start.** Weapons start at 40% Power, and V1 deals less damage to Minecraft mobs. A zombie or spider now takes two headshots at the start. Fully upgraded weapons still kill in one.
- **Fire hurts V1 less.** Standing in fire, lava or on magma does about a third of the damage it used to.
- **ULTRAKILL's fire on burning mobs**, instead of Minecraft's flames covering your view.
- **A smoother hand swing** when mining or punching with Minecraft hands.
- **Multiplayer fixes.** Commands that give P, weapons, upgrades or bosses only work for the host and operators. Guests can't stop someone else's run or send away someone else's boss. `/uc settings` opens on the player who typed it. A player who quit while down comes back normally.
- **Known issues:** a boss chasing another player doesn't hurt them yet, and duel hits may not register. Both will be fixed in the next update.
