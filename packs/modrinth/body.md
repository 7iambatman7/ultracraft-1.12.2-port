# Ultracraft

**The real ULTRAKILL, played inside Minecraft.** ULTRAKILL runs alongside Minecraft and is drawn into its window: you are V1, with ULTRAKILL's own movement, weapons, arms, style meter and enemies, and Minecraft's blocks and mobs become ULTRAKILL's world and targets.

You need to own **ULTRAKILL** (Steam) and play on **Windows**. Ultracraft doesn't contain any of ULTRAKILL's files: it runs your own copy.

## What you get
- **V1 in Minecraft**: dash, slide, slam, wall-jump and shoot through Minecraft's terrain. ULTRAKILL's weapons break blocks, its blood stains them, its explosions crater them. F8 switches between V1 and Steve, F5 shows V1 in third person.
- **ULTRAKILL's enemies in Minecraft's world**: they spawn in the dark by biome (husks on the plains, Greed's soldiers in deserts, flesh in swamps, machines in jungles; demons in the Nether, angels in the End), fight Minecraft's mobs, and drop experience.
- **Bosses**: Cerberus, V2, Gabriel, Minos Prime, Sisyphus Prime and more come for you now and then, with ULTRAKILL's difficulties and traits, their own music, and loot.
- **Arenas** for each of ULTRAKILL's layers generate in the world, with a boss waiting in each.
- **The shop** (a craftable block): spend P on weapons, variants, the Alternate weapons, and upgrades for every weapon and arm.
- **The Cyber Grind**: wave after wave through 50 arenas styled after ULTRAKILL's levels, under ULTRAKILL's own skies.
- **Style rewards, fight music** from ULTRAKILL's soundtrack, ULTRAKILL's Sandbox cheats and Ultracraft's own.
- **Multiplayer**: host with Essential or Open to LAN; other players show up as V1 with their guns, and everyone fights the same enemies and bosses.

## Installing
Install this mod and Fabric API, have Steam running, and start Minecraft. On the title screen, Ultracraft asks once to set ULTRAKILL up: it adds **BepInEx 5** (an open-source mod loader, included in this mod) and its **UltraBridge plugin** to ULTRAKILL's folder. Nothing else there is changed, and ULTRAKILL's own save and settings are never touched. After that, ULTRAKILL starts and closes with Minecraft.

The **Ultracraft modpack** sets up a full instance in one go: Ultracraft, Fabric API, Sodium and Essential.

## Performance
Two games share your graphics card. Settings → Ultracraft → Performance has a **Low-End PC Preset**, ULTRAKILL's resolution (down to 360p), effects quality, blood stains, extra gore and terrain range. Sodium and Iris work with Ultracraft.
